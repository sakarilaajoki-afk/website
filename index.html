How It Works

For Teachers

From session creation to student reports in four simple steps. No technical knowledge required.

1

Create a Session

Sign in with your teacher password, choose the course and the lesson length, and create the session. A 6-digit PIN appears.

2

Share the PIN

Students join on laptops, desktops or Chromebooks with the PIN and a first name or nickname. No app, no student accounts.

3

Launch the Game

Firms of 3–4 are set up, students take their roles and a short picture tutorial, then three rounds run automatically. Follow every firm on the live chart.

4

Review & Report

Students answer a short knowledge check and download their own report. You download the class pack: class report, every student's report and a follow-up lesson.

See the whole lesson without a class: try the Teacher Demo. No PIN, nothing saved.

What Students Do

Executive Roles

Each firm has four roles. In a firm of three, the CEO is also the Finance Director.

👔

CEO

Trades with the other CEOs and hires the staff.

📢

Sales & Marketing Director

Buys and sells with other firms and books the marketing.

⚙️

Operations Manager

Buys and sells with other firms and orders the seasonal stock.

💰

Finance Director

Trades with the other Finance Directors. Loans, VAT, investments and sabotage.

Every team decision has real financial consequences. Students learn by experiencing the results of their choices, not by reading about someone else's.

Core Features

What Happens in a Session

Three rounds. The firm with the highest net position wins.

🤝

Live Trading Between Firms

Students trade cars with other firms live and negotiate the price on a deal card. Every deal needs the other side to accept.

🏪

Wholesale, Customers & Fleet

Buy stock on Wholesale, serve customers and bid for Fleet tenders.

🏛️

HMRC VAT Returns

Finance files VAT returns under the UK margin scheme.

📈

Loans & Investments

Take a loan, repay it at any time, and invest in property and funds.

📢

Marketing, Staff & Sabotage

Book campaigns, hire staff and sabotage rival firms.

⚡

Six Market Scenarios

Scenarios rotate across a licence, so the next lesson plays differently. Optional Round 3 crisis, started by the teacher.

🖼️

Picture Tutorial

A short picture tutorial for each role before trading starts.

❓

Knowledge Check

End-of-lesson questions from the course the teacher chooses.

📊

Reports

Students download their own report and a Certificate of Completion. Optional AI picks discussion points for the class report; there is no AI grading.

About

Our Story

Built from a real lesson no classroom ever taught.

"At 17, I organised large-scale events, took real financial risks — and learned the hard way what happens when you miss a tax return."

That one mistake led to €8,400 in debt recovery. No teacher, no textbook, and no career advisor had ever mentioned that this could happen. It was a lesson no classroom had prepared me for.

Today, 48% of Gen Z already run a side hustle. Social media has made entrepreneurship feel easy — but nobody teaches the financial fundamentals that can make or break a young business.

That question — what if students could make those mistakes safely, before they cost real money? — became The Business School.

The Business School is proudly built in Finland — a country consistently ranked #1 in the world for education. Our pedagogy is founded on trust, equality, and learning by doing, and those principles are at the heart of everything we build. That foundation gives us a unique perspective on how students truly learn: not through theory alone, but through experience.

Together with our development team based in Sydney, Australia, we built a live simulation designed specifically for the classroom. This cross-continental collaboration brings together Nordic educational expertise with cutting-edge product development. Students form competing companies with real leadership roles. In one lesson, they trade, invest, negotiate, file VAT returns and respond to a crisis. Students and teachers get a report at the end.

The platform is browser-based with zero IT setup. Students join with a PIN. Teachers click one button to start. It is now in classroom use in UK schools, with US and Finnish editions coming soon.
